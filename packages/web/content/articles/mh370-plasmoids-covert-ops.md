## Introduction — The Vanishing That Defied All Explanation

Military radar tracked the aircraft deviating westward across the Malay Peninsula, northwest past Penang, and then out over the Strait of Malacca toward the Nicobar Islands. The official investigation, led by the Malaysian government with assistance from Australia, China, the United Kingdom, and the United States, concluded that MH370 crashed in the Southern Indian Ocean after exhausting its fuel.
